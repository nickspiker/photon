## NOW — bleeding, or next up

- **PT multi-packet transfers livelock on one-directional UDP — PT ACKs have no relay leg (CONVICTED 2026-08-31; re-read on main 2026-11-22, still true)**: the receiver's SPEC ACK (status.rs ~5944), DATA ACK and COMPLETE (~2188-2192) go raw UDP to `src_addr` only, no NAK is ever sent (`missing_sequences` has no caller), and DATA retransmits carry no relay (pt/mod.rs ~1201), so a SPEC ACK that lands followed by lost DATA ACKs fails the transfer after 30 s or 10 dry rounds. The relay leg CANNOT be added receiver-only: ACK/COMPLETE are unsigned and a relayed one arrives as `RELAY_ADDR`, where `handle_ack`/`handle_spec_ack` (mod.rs ~611-630, ~805-824) would retarget the transfer to 0.0.0.0:0 and the pump answers to `src_addr`. Fix as one step: the sender never locks onto `RELAY_ADDR` and matches COMPLETE/NAK by stream+hash from it; the receiver relays the SPEC ACK and COMPLETE (the signer is `InboundTransfer.sender`), and DATA ACKs only once the transfer counts duplicates (the in-thread sign our ACKs are lost); `relay_send` is one HTTPS POST per frame, so never per chunk.
