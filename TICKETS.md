## TRANSPORT — direct-path hardening (ordered; land + 2-device test each)

- **Sibling gossip as the middle address tier**: ask a reachable sibling for a peer's `PeerRecord` before falling back to the seed registry. The registry tier landed 2026-07-31 (`pb_put`/`pb_get`, self-signed records, verified live); this closes the remaining single-point dependency on a device's OWN fgtw reachability — the failure that stranded a whole ceremony when 4/7 fgtw fetches failed on cellular. Also retire the 15s full-resume pulse (it re-runs the whole attest; should be announce-only or doorbell-driven).
- **TCP as a direct path (T1.3)**: `tcp.rs` has no inbound accept — TCP is only a whole-payload PT fallback to a known UDP address. Add an inbound listener on `PHOTON_PORT` feeding the same receiver pipeline, plus a TCP connect raced alongside the UDP punch. Wins UDP-blocked networks and makes a forwarded host just work. Test: UDP-blocked client reaches a forwarded host.
- **IPv6 as first-class (T3)**: `gather.rs` prioritises HostV6 but full local-interface enumeration is deferred and the announce may carry no global v6. Biggest single win for a cellular peer on a v6 carrier — bypasses CGNAT entirely. Test: two v6 hosts connect with zero punch.
- **Server-coordinated simultaneous-open (T2)**: targeted WS delivery in the worker (subscribe by device_pubkey) + a punch-request message, so a cold pair connects on the first attempt. NEEDS A WORKER DEPLOY.
- **NAT relay tier**: symmetric↔symmetric (two cellular peers) has no direct path at all. Friend-fleet devices as relays fits the peers-are-fgtw direction.
- **Mac wake for messages/calls** (LOW, approved 2026-08-21): stage 1 Wake-on-LAN sibling knock (capture MACs during LAN discovery, magic packet → ~3-5s → TCP knock); stage 2 Bonjour sleep-proxy registration of `_photon._tcp` (covers friend traffic, needs an Apple proxy device present). LAN-only either way; hibernated Macs wake for nothing.

- **One-directional-path doctrine (2026-07-31)** — keep applying it: three subsystems died the same death in one day (blind srv answers, message ACKs, history page serves) — a validated path whose REVERSE direction silently drops. Rule: small idempotent response frames ALWAYS carry their relay copy alongside any direct leg. Validation proves OUR probes round-trip, never that the reverse delivers. Suspect the same mechanism in punch-probe replies.

- **Relay policy convergence — escalation, not promiscuity** (Nick approved 2026-08-06, land after the field round): the relay is a metadata oracle, so parallel copies of big payloads double exactly the traffic whose cost is material. End state: unconfirmable-and-small (ACKs, ClutchComplete, weave probe — no failure edge exists) go parallel always; confirmable (chat, anything ACKed) go direct-first on a trusted path with the FIRST retransmit carrying the relay copy; large (offers, avatars, history pages) never parallel — fast-fail direct, then relay-only.
