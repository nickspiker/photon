## DESIGN — decided, unbuilt (home docs carry the detail)

- **Fleet-assisted attest** ("I hold the vault, my friend holds the key", designed 2026-08-07): auto-attest after reboot with NO durable identity secret at rest. The capsule is sealed under a random `wake_key` the device does NOT keep; each sibling holds a share in its own vault under the fleet key. Boot → derive device keypair from the machine fact → signed WAKE_REQUEST over relay + LAN → sibling grants iff signature valid AND in current fold AND not locked → open, attest, ROTATE immediately (a captured share is single-use). Critical constraint: NEVER seal the capsule under a machine fact — `identity_seed`'s handle derivation is deliberately cheap, so a stolen durable capsule falls to a dictionary sweep and then passes the lockout's handle gate.
