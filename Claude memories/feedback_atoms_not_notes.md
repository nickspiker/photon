Nick 2026-10-27: "Don't call atoms notes plz" — after I described the self-only conversation as "your notes … live under Atoms" in the v132 notes and replies.

**Why:** atoms are a general thing (you can found as many as you like, titled or untitled, and bind people in later); "notes" names one use of one of them and makes atoms sound like a notes feature.
**How to apply:** in replies, release notes and new docs say "atom" (or "the conversation with only you in it" for the [us,us] one); no rename sweep of existing code/docs unasked ([[feedback_photons_are_messages]] style). Related: [[project_conversation_convergence]].
