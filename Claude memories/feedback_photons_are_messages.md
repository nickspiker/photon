Nick 2026-10-06: *"I've officially decided to call messages on photon, Photons."*

**Why:** the house names its surfaces with exact words — wave = a sound wave, beam = a beam of light, and a photon is the quantum of light: one indivisible unit that arrives whole or not at all, which is what a message in a lane is. The lexicon entry (docs/lexicon.md, "photon — adopted 2026-10-06") holds the reasoning; the 1960 dictionary page is not yet shot.

**How to apply:** in prose, docs, tickets, memories and user-facing copy written from now on, a message is a photon (plural photons). Do NOT run a code/UI/wire rename sweep unasked — "wave" was a deliberate flag day (identifiers, strings in 16 languages, wire bytes); whether "photon" gets the same treatment is Nick's call. Code-sense "message" in protocol names (CHAT_MESSAGE, MESSAGE_ACK) stays until he says otherwise. Careful with ambiguity: "Photon" capitalised is the app.
